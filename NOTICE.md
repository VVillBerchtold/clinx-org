## Drug names

The names in `reference/fda-ndc/names.csv` are taken from the National Drug Code Directory, a public file of the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cder/ndctext.zip). `reference/fda-ndc/SOURCE.json` records the day it was downloaded and the SHA-256 of the file. The text is kept as the directory lists it. The FDA has not reviewed or endorsed this site, and a listing in the directory does not mean the FDA has approved a drug.
